# SUMMA assistant: reference answers from the Pacioli support team (set 9)

Short official answers maintained by the SUMMA support team for the virtual assistant.

## How to create a recovery order for fines?

All the instructions are situated here. https://eceuropaeu.sharepoint.com/sites/budgpedia/SitePages/recovery-fines.aspx

## How to encode a pre-financing?

Use tile "Create Supplier Down Payment Request" transaction code F-47

## How to find/see an attached document?

To review the attachment, click on the “Services for Object”. Afterwards, press the “Attachment list” to check one of the documents.

## How to know if a payment has been executed?

Please consult the tile manage supply line items and check if your document has been cleared.

## What are the possible types of PO managed in SUMMA?

ZDC=ProcContract-Direct ZGA=Grant Agreement ZOLC=Other Legal Commit. ZSC-ProcContractSpecific ZSH=Shared Management

## What does the word "SUMMA" mean?

SUMMA is not an acronym! SUMMA takes its name from "Summa de arithmetica, geometria, proportioni et proportionalita" written by my namesake Luca Pacioli. In "Summa de arithmetica" Pacioli first codifies the double entry accounting, which was already known since ancient Egypt.

## Where can I find the accounts assignment field in an invoice?

There is no account assignment field selectable in the invoice.

## how to change the supplier in a purchase order?

It is currently not possible to change the supplier of a purchase order. Please contact the summa helpdesk.

## what does DPR mean?

DPR means Down Payment Request.
